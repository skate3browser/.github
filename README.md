## Skate 3 Browser Trick Guide

### Ollie

The Ollie is the foundation for most other tricks.

On keyboard:

**↓ then ↑**

Pull down to prepare the board and flick upward to pop into the air.

Once the basic Ollie becomes comfortable, start using it to clear gaps, jump onto ledges, and approach rails.

### Nollie

For a nose-based pop:

**↑ then ↓**

The movement reverses the basic Ollie direction and shifts the pop toward the front of the board.

### Kickflip

Start with an Ollie-style downward input, then flick toward the upper-left or left direction.

The board rotates along its axis while the skater is airborne.

### Heelflip

Use a downward input followed by an upper-right or right flick.

It creates the opposite board rotation to a kickflip.

### Grinds

Approach a rail or ledge with enough speed, Ollie onto the obstacle, and maintain the appropriate stick or directional angle to lock into the grind.

Good grinds depend on both your approach and your landing position rather than the trick input alone.

### Manuals

Instead of fully popping the board, use a lighter backward input to balance on the rear wheels.

Manuals are especially useful for connecting separate tricks and obstacles into a continuous line.
